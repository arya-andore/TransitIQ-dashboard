Definitions & data quality
Amounts are shown in source currency units: no currency field is supplied. Transaction amount totals are gross recorded amounts, not revenue or settled cash flow, and include failed, reversed and pending transactions unless filtered. Fraud is the supplied is_fraud flag, not a model prediction; flagged amount does not mean confirmed loss. Success and fraud rates use transaction counts. Unique customers are distinct IDs in the selected scope.
All 75,000 rows and 20 source columns are loaded. Transaction IDs are unique. No missing values except 35,735 loan-type cells; all correspond to records with has_loan = 0. Account balances and EMI values are not summed because repeated customer-level observations could double-count exposure. Customer attributes may vary across synthetic records. This dashboard describes synthetic data and is not a live banking report.
